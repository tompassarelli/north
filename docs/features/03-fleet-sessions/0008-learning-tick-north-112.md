- Learning tick (north#112): `north tune` decides, from the ledger's recently closed boxes, the cells briefs and routing read: `route CATEGORY` (the arm dispatch takes once both arms hold enough runs) and `eta_minutes` and `budget_usd` per family and category. It writes them to `~/.local/state/north/tune/cells.json`, which dispatch and `north fleet launch` follow while fresh, and logs each changed cell with its evidence to `provenance.jsonl`. Cells whose cost per closed box rose sharply week on week share one rolling issue behind the two-breach gate (opened on the second risen tick, edited only on change, closed when calm), a section per cell listing its closed boxes with their worker runs (north#579). It also prints, per tier and category, the median and p90 ratio of actual to estimated minutes and cost of the week's runs and the share inside their estimate (`n/a` for cost while runs record no expected spend), and files one issue under the same rule for each whose median left 0.67-1.5x over three or more runs. It prints the share of the last day's finished runs that recorded each essential field and fails when one is under 98% (north#255). `--dry-run` writes and files nothing.
