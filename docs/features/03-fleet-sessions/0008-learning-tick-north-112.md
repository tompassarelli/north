It writes them to `~/.local/state/north/tune/cells.json`, which dispatch and `north fleet launch` follow while it is fresh, and appends each changed cell with its evidence, interval and rerun command to `provenance.jsonl` beside it. A tier and category whose cost per closed box rose sharply week on week gets one north issue unless an open issue of that title holds it; the issue lists each closed box behind the figure with its worker runs (session, tier, category, start and cost), so the rise traces from the issue on a host that cannot read the ledger (north#579). It also prints, per tier and category, the median and p90 ratio of actual to estimated minutes and cost of the week's runs and the share inside their estimate (`n/a` for cost while runs record no expected spend), and files one north issue under the same rule for each whose median ratio left 0.67-1.5x over at least three runs. `--dry-run` writes and files nothing.
