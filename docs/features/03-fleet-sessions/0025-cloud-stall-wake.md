- Cloud stall wake: `north cloud wait` treats a finished turn with no `Done:`, `Not done:`, `Split:` or `Blocked:` line in its last reply and no patch handback as a stall, such as a worker that ended its turn on a background build; it posts a continue message telling the worker to run that work in the foreground, finish and hand back the patch with a report, then waits again after that message, at most twice, before printing that the worker stalled and exiting 1.
