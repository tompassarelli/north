- Stranded lanes (north#333): `north fleet stranded --json` lists this registry's exited or parked sessions whose last reply names `Stop: capacity` or ended on a wait (no Done, Not done, Split, Stop or Blocked opening and no question, as the wait wake reads it; a `Done:` report names neither), with each linked worktree of its checkout that report names by path, directory or branch, read here or over ssh on its machine after fetching origin/main: commits ahead of origin/main, uncommitted or untracked changes, and whether merging it into origin/main changes nothing. Each `north dispatch` run reads it from every host before it places boxes. A session whose named lanes hold nothing origin/main lacks is forgotten (`north fleet forget`). Any other holds its `--task` items, so no fresh launch starts beside it, and is revived (`north fleet revive`, whose placement admits it as a launch, with `--local` when its conversation lives on its registry's host and a `--pin` when placement would rank another account first, then `north fleet send` with the turn that names its lanes) once its machine's saturation refusal (admission queue, root disk, five-minute load) is clear and its account is open, signed in there and has a free slot and room, as a launch needs; a session is revived once per report (`~/.local/state/agents/dispatch/revived.json`). `north flow` lists each repository's stranded lanes stopped at least 15 minutes ago with their stop (`stranded N lanes: SESSION LANE (STOP, AGE)`), and `--stall-alarm` sends the Fleet parent and the proxy `Lanes stranded in REPO` every 15 minutes while any remain. Refused land branches: `north flow` reads the `autoland` commit status of every unlanded `claude/land-*` tip, in any repository whose Autoland posts one (wisp and makgora), counts a failed or errored tip as dead rather than pending, and lists it under `refused` with the session safe-push's push log names as its pusher, the refusal and the run link; while that pusher is no live Fleet session, `--stall-alarm` sends the Fleet parent and the proxy `Land branches Autoland refused in REPO` every 15 minutes until the branch is fixed or withdrawn. A live pusher hears the refusal from its own safe-push wait.
