- Week projection (north#255): `north dispatch` keeps a box off an account whose measured week burn takes the week to 100% before the box's ETA or the week's reset, and names that projection in the held line. Each launch in `launches.jsonl` records its account's five-hour and week used percent projected at its ETA. `north tune` prints the p50 and p90 error in points, and the bias, of both projections over the week against the first usage reading within 15 minutes after each ETA; a window that resets before the ETA is not scored, and an unreadable usage ledger fails the tick.
