- Live claims: `threads claim`, `threads list` and `north dispatch` share one claim rule in `crates/north-threads/src/threads/claims`. A claim is void when it predates its issue, its issue is closed or missing, or its owner is no live fleet session, and stale when its owner has been silent past its ETA (`overdue_ratio`); dispatch holds neither, and `claim` takes either over with an event naming why. Any other claim is live: `claim` refuses another owner and dispatch holds the issue. `threads ready`, `threads unowned` and `north work list` read the same list, and every mutating `north fleet` command has the claims of exited sessions released (Exited-claims sweep).
