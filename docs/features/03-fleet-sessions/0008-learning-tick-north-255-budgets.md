- Budget scoring (north#255): each worker run stores the Budget its brief set at launch (the `at $X API-equivalent spend` line drawn at E's p90) in `runs.budget_usd`, kept through completion. `north tune` prints, over 7 d, the runs carrying a stored Budget and per category n, the share of runs over their Budget and the actual/E p50 and p90; once a category holds 30 such runs its `budget_usd` cells take their multiplier from that p90 (times the multiplier the Budgets were set with, read back as the median Budget over the median spend, held between 1x and 4x because most runs spend pennies and their p90 is a few runs' tail) instead of the constant 2x.
