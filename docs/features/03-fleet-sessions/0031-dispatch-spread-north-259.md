- Dispatch spread (north#259): one `north fleet dispatch` run starts no box on an account at its provider's limit or declared the operational reserve, and no more on a host than its free `resident_cap` slots and the builds its admission queue takes before reaching the limit placement skips it at, both recounted after every launch in the run. A host whose admission queue is at that limit, or whose root disk is over 85% in use, takes no session from dispatch or `north fleet launch`; `--dry-run` prints each host's refusal and a `skip` line naming the bound that stopped each box ([rationale](../../rationale/dispatch-spread.md)).
