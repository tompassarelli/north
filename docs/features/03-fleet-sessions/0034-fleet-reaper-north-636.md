- Fleet reaper (north#636): the fleet tick's last probe, `probes.settle.reap`, retires an idle or parked session (not the proxy, an adopted window or a verify-north session) that nothing would wake (no registered wait, open gate, queued land ticket, live child, job lease or running process in its scope) when its newest final report, with no new instruction after it, was taken by its parent and opens `Done:` with every commit it names on origin/main, or `Not done:` or `Split:` with a `Stop:` line; or when it has been quiet past `fleet_stale_hours`. Retiring writes `retired.json` (why, when, last reply) and moves the session directory and transcript under `.archive` with `ended: retired`; no worktree is touched. A message or row to a retired name goes to its nearest live ancestor, else the proxy, as one turn, never reviving it, and is logged in `.dead-letters.jsonl`. `north fleet reap --dry-run` prints each verdict; each tick writes `.reap.json`, the `fleet-retire` loop's record.
