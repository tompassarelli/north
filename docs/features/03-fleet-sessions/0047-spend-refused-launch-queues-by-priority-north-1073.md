- Spend-refused launches queue by priority (north#1073): `north fleet launch` keeps a launch placement refuses only on its account's spend in the host's launch queue under `~/.local/state/north/fleet/.launch-queue` instead of failing, prints `queued NAME`, and exits 0; the fleet tick retries the oldest first, urgent before normal, and starts it when a slot frees. `--priority urgent|normal` sets the place, defaulting to normal, so `north fleet launch --priority urgent` goes ahead of dispatch's launches.
