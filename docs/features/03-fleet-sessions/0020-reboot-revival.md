- Reboot revival: `north fleet rebooted` lists this host's sessions a reboot stranded (parked with their process gone, last written before the boot, active within their idle limit then, and not ended on a `Done:` report), and `--revive` restarts each through `north fleet revive` with a message telling it to resume from its last checkpoint. `north fleet tick` runs it for its own host, so a rebooted host's sessions resume on its first tick and one that cannot be revived is retried on the next.
