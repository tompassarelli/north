- Coordinator break glass (north#1106, north#1626): `north fleet break-glass --issue REPO#N REASON...` lifts the coordinator's reach guard (`coordinator_reach`) for `break_glass_minutes` (30, the most it can be) and only when the harness itself measures an incident: the last two launches in `~/.local/state/agents/dispatch/launches.jsonl` both failed, `north runtime probe launch claude` exits non-zero, the laptop's capacity watchdog reports an active host-overload episode (`$CAPACITY_WATCHDOG_DIR/overload.json`, default `~/.local/state/agents/watchdog/overload.json`, has `reported: true`), or the coordinator host booted in the last 30 minutes. With no such incident it refuses, records nothing and lifts nothing. A grant records who (the fleet session or user), why and when in `~/.local/state/north/break-glass.jsonl`, appends a box to the issue that tracks the cause, and prints in `north gate digest` (the digest the gate-digest hook shows the owner first) with its expiry; the lift ends by itself at the 30 minutes.
- Coordinator break glass for a landing stall: `north fleet break-glass --reason TEXT` needs no issue and lifts the coordinator's reach guard (coordinator_reach) while `north ledger flow --stall-alarm` reports a fresh landing stall in north: the stall episode in `~/.local/state/agents/flow/lander-stalls.json` is the one granted, so the lift ends after those minutes, when that stall clears, or when its record stops being fresh, whichever comes first, and does not carry into a later stall. It opens no box, since the `landing is stalled` issue the alarm files already tracks it.
