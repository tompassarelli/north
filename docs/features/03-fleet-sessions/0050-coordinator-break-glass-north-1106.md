- Coordinator break glass (north#1106): `north fleet break-glass --issue REPO#N REASON...` lifts the coordinator's read-streak budget (`coordinator_read_streak`) for `break_glass_minutes` (30, the most it can be) and only when the harness itself measures that workers cannot start: the last two launches in `~/.local/state/agents/dispatch/launches.jsonl` both failed, or `north runtime probe launch claude` exits non-zero. While a worker can launch it refuses, records nothing and lifts nothing. A grant records who (the fleet session or user), why and when in `~/.local/state/north/break-glass.jsonl`, appends a box to the issue that tracks the cause, and prints in `north gate digest` (the digest the gate-digest hook shows the owner first) with its expiry; the lift ends by itself at the 30 minutes.
- Coordinator break glass for a landing stall: `north fleet break-glass --reason TEXT` needs no issue and lifts the coordinator's reach guard (coordinator_reach) while `north ledger flow --stall-alarm` reports a stalled lander: the stall episode in `~/.local/state/agents/flow/lander-stalls.json` is the one granted, so the lift ends when that stall clears and does not carry into a later stall. It opens no box, since the `landing is stalled` issue the alarm files already tracks it.
