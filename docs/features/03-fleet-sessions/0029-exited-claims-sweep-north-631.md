- Exited-claims sweep (north#631): a mutating `north fleet` verb other than `launch` that finds an exited session no sweep has cleared starts `north fleet sweep-claims` in the background and returns without waiting on the ledger or warning about it. The sweep clears those sessions' claims by owner, at most 64 owners per `threads holder` read and 30 s per ledger call, and marks each swept session with `claims.released` in its session directory so no later sweep asks about it until it starts anew; a session revived during a sweep stays unmarked. One sweep runs per fleet at a time, holding `.claims-sweep.log` in the fleet directory locked and writing its outcome or error there.
