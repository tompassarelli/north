- Exited-claims sweep (north#631): a mutating `north fleet` verb other than `launch` that finds an exited session no sweep has cleared starts `north fleet sweep-claims` in the background and returns without waiting on the ledger or warning about it. The sweep clears those sessions' claims by owner, at most 64 owners per `north threads holder` read and 30 s per ledger call, and marks each swept session with `claims.released` in its session directory so no later sweep asks about it until it starts anew; a session revived during a sweep stays unmarked. One sweep runs per fleet at a time, holding `.claims-sweep.log` in the fleet directory locked and writing its outcome or error there.
- Claims of launches that never start (north#1070): a launch whose session fails anywhere between its claim and its first turn releases every claim it took before it reports the failure. A session whose record names no host ten minutes after its launch, and a fleet-table row still `starting` ten minutes after it last updated, count as exited: the sweep and a launch's holder check release their claims, the live-owner list leaves them out, and the Claude spend cap and a machine's resident cap do not count their rows. `north threads claim --box TEXT` treats two boxes of one issue as one box when one's slug is the other's leading whole words, so a coordinator's `--box "Pinned runtime"` and dispatch's slug of the whole box refuse each other, naming the holder.
