- Offload eviction: each build host's lease reaper (every 30 s) keeps the root disk with wide hysteresis from the stores `harness/disk-ledger.toml` gives its own passes, in the ledger's order. Passing the high watermark (free under `disk_high_watermark_free_gib`, else 13% of a smaller disk) starts one cleanup pass that takes idle build-offload slots, idle shared cargo target output, wisp window builds unchanged for 20 minutes and the oldest sccache entries, past their caps, until the disk is back over the low watermark (`disk_low_watermark_free_gib`, else 40%) or nothing regenerable is left; the pass records one `host-disk` incident and pages the proxy once with what it took and the ledger's three largest stores, and the next pass waits until the disk has come back over the high watermark and crossed it again. Between passes only caps are kept, build-offload slots included. Land-offload slots are held together, the lander's reserved slot last, to the host's `land_offload` budget on every pass, and the slot warmer fills no cold slot past it; past the budget, only a pass that starts with the host closed to dispatch for disk takes them: under the eviction line (`offload_evict_free_gib`, else 20%), or under the host's own `disk_free_gib` dispatch bound in orchestration.toml when that is higher. Every such pass is a cleanup pass that takes the caches first, then land slots oldest first, the lander's last, never one whose `.lock` a job holds, pruning each removed worktree's registration, until the disk is back over the eviction stop line (`offload_evict_stop_free_gib`, else 25%), or over the dispatch bound by the same margin, so a pass never leaves the disk on the line placement refuses at; a host past its dispatch bound also starts the episode, and when the pass leaves it still closed, the page says so and names the stores holding the space. The slot warmer fills no cold slot that would take the disk under that same line. Every pass also deletes a `target.seeding-<pid>` copy under `~/code/*/main` or `~/code/*/worktrees/*` whose process is gone and whose directory is over an hour old, so a seeding process killed past its cleanup leaves no build output behind, and drops the git-ignored `target/` of each lane a landed ticket in this host's land queue, outbox or records mirror names, once the lane still sits at the ticket's head, the commit the lander made is on main, the worktree is not locked and no process has its cwd, root or an open file in the lane: a session that exits or retires after its landing leaves its build directory for at most one pass, and `north lane reclaim` makes the same release.
