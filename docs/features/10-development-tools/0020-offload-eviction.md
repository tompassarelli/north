- Offload eviction: each build host's lease reaper (every 30 s) keeps the root disk with wide hysteresis from the stores `harness/disk-ledger.toml` gives its own passes, in the ledger's order. Passing the high watermark (free under `disk_high_watermark_free_gib`, else 13% of a smaller disk) starts one cleanup pass that takes idle build-offload slots, idle shared cargo target output, wisp window builds unchanged for 20 minutes and the oldest sccache entries, past their caps, until the disk is back over the low watermark (`disk_low_watermark_free_gib`, else 40%) or nothing regenerable is left; the pass records one `host-disk` incident and pages the proxy once with what it took and the ledger's three largest stores, and the next pass waits until the disk has come back over the high watermark and crossed it again. Between passes only caps are kept, build-offload slots included. Only under the eviction line (`offload_evict_free_gib`, else 20%) does it take land-offload slots, after the caches, the lander's last, never one whose `.lock` a job holds, pruning each removed worktree's registration.
