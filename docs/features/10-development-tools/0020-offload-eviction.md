- Offload eviction: each build host's lease reaper (every 30 s) keeps the root disk with wide hysteresis from the stores `harness/disk-ledger.toml` gives its own passes, in the ledger's order. The reaper holds the disk over one line, its held line: the line the host closes to new work under, its own `disk_free_gib` dispatch bound in orchestration.toml when that is higher than the eviction line (`offload_evict_free_gib`, else 20%), else the eviction line, plus the margin the eviction stop line (`offload_evict_stop_free_gib`, else 25%) keeps over the eviction line. Every pass that starts under it is a cleanup pass: it takes idle build-offload slots, idle shared cargo target output, wisp window builds unchanged for 20 minutes and the oldest sccache entries, past their caps, toward the low watermark (`disk_low_watermark_free_gib`, else 40%), and then land slots past the host's `land_offload` budget oldest first, the lander's last, never one whose `.lock` a job holds, pruning each removed worktree's registration, until the disk is back over the held line, so a host closes for disk only once nothing its rules may reclaim is left. The first such pass records one `host-disk` incident and pages the proxy once with what it took and the ledger's three largest stores, and when the pass leaves the host closed, the page says so and names the stores holding the space; the next page waits until the disk has come back over the held line and crossed it again. Between passes only caps are kept, build-offload slots included. Land-offload slots are held together, the lander's reserved slot last, to the host's `land_offload` budget on every pass, and the slot warmer fills no cold slot past it nor one that would take the disk under the held line. Every pass also deletes a `target.seeding-<pid>` copy under `~/code/*/main` or `~/code/*/worktrees/*` whose process is gone and whose directory is over an hour old, so a seeding process killed past its cleanup leaves no build output behind, and drops the git-ignored `target/` of each lane a landed ticket in this host's land queue, outbox or records mirror names, once the lane still sits at the ticket's head, the commit the lander made is on main, the worktree is not locked and no process has its cwd, root or an open file in the lane: a session that exits or retires after its landing leaves its build directory for at most one pass, and `north lane reclaim` makes the same release.
