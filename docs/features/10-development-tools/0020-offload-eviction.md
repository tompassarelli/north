- Offload eviction: each build host's lease reaper keeps the root disk above the line the host closes to new work under, taking back what `harness/disk-ledger.toml` lists in its order: idle build slots, idle shared cargo output, stale window builds and old sccache entries past their caps, then land slots beyond the host's `land_offload` budget, never one a job holds. A host closes for disk only once nothing its rules may reclaim is left; the first pass under the line records a `host-disk` incident and pages the coordinator once with what it took and the largest stores. Every pass also deletes `target.seeding-<pid>` copies whose seeding process is gone and the build output of lanes whose landing is on main, as `north lane reclaim` does. The lines are `offload_evict_free_gib`, `offload_evict_stop_free_gib` and `disk_low_watermark_free_gib` in `harness/defaults.toml`.
