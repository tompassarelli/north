- Retention: `north retain`, run on the laptop, archives the GPU host's expired makgora evidence, capture batches and unpinned build-input store versions into `~/archive/makgora-gex45/`, verifying every file's sha256 against the source before it deletes the source, and stops starting new batches once its time budget is spent. Ages and budget are the `evidence_retention_hours`, `capture_batch_retention_hours`, `build_input_retention_hours` and `retention_budget_minutes` keys in `harness/defaults.toml`. A folder holding a `PINNED` file, held open by a process, holding a socket, fifo or device, or whose copy differs is kept, and when makgora's pins cannot be read no store version is archived. Flags are in `north retain --help`.
