- Fact graph (north#669): `north graph tell|retract T1 T2 T3`, `show T1`, `ask QUERY_JSON` and `validate` (flags `--db PATH`, `--writer NAME`, `--json`) keep connected facts in `~/.local/state/north/graph.db`, a SQLite WAL store that serializes writers from many processes. The kernel is Beagle Store's: Atoms (String, Int, Float, Bool, Keyword, Instant) have identity by kind and canonical payload (one NaN, `+0.0` apart from `-0.0`), a Triple of Terms nests in any position, and every `tell` or `retract` records an occurrence with its writer. A retraction withdraws the newest live equal assertion; one with no live match is recorded with `stateChanged false`. `ask` runs Beagle's structured rule query over `triple`, `occurrence`, `withdrawal`, `assertion` and `quoted` in a Rust Datalog engine (recursion, stratified negation, arithmetic, aggregates, step and time budgets). Terms, the query JSON, the schema and the 1M-occurrence timing are in `docs/graph.md`.
