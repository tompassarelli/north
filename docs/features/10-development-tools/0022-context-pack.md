- Context pack (north#873): `north work brief pack [--issue REF]... PATH...` prints, in at most 3,000 characters, for each path a brief's `Files:` names: its public items with their lines, its structure.toml module and the modules it may use, the files that changed with it most often in 90 days and any revert of one of its commits within 48 h, and the `cargo nextest` filter for the tests the coverage map says reach it (a named list for three tests or fewer, else the binaries holding them; the crate's tests when the host has no coverage map). It also prints what the fact graph holds on the file (tests that reach it, files its commits also touched, quoted unverified claims, correction classes), read-only from graph.db and cut off 120 ms in; a missing or locked graph.db costs that section only. Dispatch appends it to the brief of a box on the context-pack arm.
