- Verb help: `north VERB --help` or `-h`, at any depth such as `north capacity watchdog sample --help`, is answered by the dispatcher before any verb runs, from the usage the verb publishes (safe-push, fleet, cloud, capacity watchdog, land serve, lane-gc) or else from the verb's lines in `north --help`, so a probe never samples, posts or launches; every verb in the CLI verb table has a `north --help` line, and a test asks each for `--help` and `-h` under a 5 s deadline. A `--help` after `--`, or after the program a wrapper runs (`capacity gate`, `resident-cap gate`, `gh-session`, `session-unit`, `ssh-session`, `ssh-argv`, `git-credential`, `threads`), goes to that program. It exists because on 2026-11-06 firn's unit-flags probe of `north capacity watchdog sample --help` sampled for 42 minutes and held a rebuild, and `north eta-calibration --help` posted its weekly comment (north#660); firn-tools unit-flags now also kills a probe past 10 s and names it.
