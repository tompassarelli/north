- Goals as checks (north#641): each project (north, makgora, wisp) keeps `goals.toml` at its root, one `[[goal]]` per goal drawn from its README or AGENTS.md, with the line it comes from, an oracle command that prints one number, a `max` or `min` bound, and a class. A `contract` goal is never red and a landing that turns it red is reverted; a `debt` goal may be red up to its `ceiling` while its `owner` and `revisit` trigger stand in for the `Target-debt:` record (docs/rationale/targets.md). `north goals check [--root DIR] [PROJECT...]` runs every oracle (bash -o pipefail) side by side under 8 s each and prints each goal with its measured value, source, oracle, class, ceiling and green or red; it exits 1 on a red contract, a goal over its ceiling or an oracle that gave no number. `north land profile [--repo NAME] --metric landing-p50|landing-p90|verification-p50|verification-p90` is the oracle for landing and verification times. The fleet tick tells the proxy `goals red (N): ...` when a goal turns red, when the red set changes and every 6 h while it stays red, reading oracles at most every 10 minutes (`~/.local/state/north/goals/tick.json`).
