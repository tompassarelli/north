- Goals as checks (north#641): each project (north, makgora, wisp) keeps `concerns.toml` at its root, one `[[concern]]` per goal drawn from its README or AGENTS.md, with the line it comes from, an oracle command that prints one number, a `max` or `min` bound, a class, and `reads`, `commit` when the oracle measures the checked-out tree or `host` (the default) when it reads this host's state. A `contract` goal is never red; the lander no longer attributes or reverts a landing for one (north#1650). An oracle that exits non-zero, times out or prints no number gives no verdict and reverts nothing and one that answers a tree two ways is named nondeterministic. A `debt` goal may be red up to its `ceiling` while its `owner` and `revisit` trigger stand in for the `Target-debt:` record. `north check concerns [--root DIR] [PROJECT...]` runs every oracle prints value, source, oracle, class and verdict, and exits 1 on a red contract, a goal over its ceiling or an oracle with no number; `north land profile --metric landing-p50|landing-p90|landing-projected|verification-p50|verification-p90` measures landing times; landing-projected, the last landings' service time times 1 or 2, is what the debt ceiling reads. The fleet tick tells the coordinator `goals red (N): ...` when the red set changes and every 6 h while it stays red.
