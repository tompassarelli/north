- Hook verdict stats: `north ledger worker` ingest records every PreToolUse and PostToolUse hook refusal, rewrite and advisory it finds in Claude transcripts into the ledger's `hook_events` table, and `north hook stats [--since 7d]` prints per rule its fires and repeats and the candidates that follow. A `hook_events` row holds session, tool call, tool, stable rule id from `guard/hook_rules.rs`, time and call ordinal. `hook stats` prints per rule its fires, sessions, refusals, rewrites, advisories, in-session repeats and median calls lost to a repeat, then auto-fix candidates (mechanical rules still refusing), skill-text candidates (most-repeated rules with the skill line that should have prevented them), and prune candidates (no fire in 30 days). A column beside them prints each refusing rule's false share from its last fixed sample in `crates/north-guard/src/guard/hook_refusal_samples.toml` (up to 30 labelled rows for every rule that refused from 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-08), or `unmeasured` when the rule has none, and a closing list names each rule over 10% false with its `narrowed` line or "not narrowed" (docs/rationale/guard-false-refusals.md).
