- Experiment registry: `docs/rationale/experiments.toml` holds one entry per live policy or routing change (its landed sha, the north flow outcome metric it should move, the predicted direction and bar, the before and after windows, the review date and the owner issue). `north experiments judge [--root DIR] [--today YYYY-MM-DD] [--file]` judges each entry due on or before today from the threads ledger's finished worker runs and their 48 h rework verdicts: keep when the metric moved the predicted way by at least the bar, revert when it moved as far the other way, decide (one line for Tom with the numbers and a recommendation) in between, and re-measure when a reading is missing or rests on fewer samples than the entry's `min_n`. With `--file` it appends each verdict once as a box on the owner issue (keep ticked; revert, decide and re-measure open, reopening a closed issue) and remembers it in `~/.local/state/north/experiments/filed`; `north flow --stall-alarm` runs it every five minutes, and it reads the ledger only when an unfiled entry is due. `north experiments check [--root DIR]` (a `.safe-push` land check on `docs/rationale/`) refuses a landing when a rationale note's review rule (an `Overturned by:` paragraph or a `## Review` section) has no entry quoting it exactly, or an entry quotes a rule its note no longer holds.
