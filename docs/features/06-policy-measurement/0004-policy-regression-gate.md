- Policy regression gate: `agents sync` measures the candidate policy's startup tokens (chars/4 of global and code-root policy, the Claude skill and agent listings and the largest SessionStart role output; the harness-bundled listing and project AGENTS.md files are not counted) and the sha256 of `south:agents/AGENTS.md`, and compares them with `south:agents/operating-point.toml` and the newest `sweep-*.jsonl` row for that sha256 in `south:agents/replay/` or `~/.local/state/agents/replay/` with 3 or more trials on every scenario the operating point's row measured (a later 1-trial row or a newly added scenario does not displace it). It refuses to publish with no operating point, when the live policy has no such sweep row, when that row ran on another model than the point, flips a scenario the point passed every trial to 0 passes, or shows a conformance drop (one-sided Fisher exact, alpha 0.05), or when startup tokens grew without a significant conformance gain; otherwise it prints `session start N tokens (operating point M at DATE, conformance C)`, the token breakdown, SKILL body lines and Codex listing characters, and `sweep due` when tokens differ from the operating point by more than 15%. It never runs replays; `policy-replay.py --sweep` does, weekly from the Monday tick and on each new model release `model-watch` reports. The `agent-config-check` rules group requires every `rules.toml` entry to carry a `placement` (`global`, `merged:<id>`, `skill:<name>`, `hook:<name>` or `dropped`), matches the `global` entries 1:1 and in order with the AGENTS.md bullets, and fails an entry added since the landed `origin/main` copy unless it cites at least 2 ids in `incidents.jsonl`. `safe-push` runs the same sha256 lookup at landing: a change that alters `agents/AGENTS.md` is refused unless the pushed tree carries an `agents/replay/sweep-*.jsonl` row for the new sha256 with 3 or more trials on every `agents/replay/*.json` scenario, and the refusal prints the `policy-replay.py --sweep` command and its measured cost (about 9 minutes and $2 at list price); a change that leaves `agents/AGENTS.md` alone is untouched.
