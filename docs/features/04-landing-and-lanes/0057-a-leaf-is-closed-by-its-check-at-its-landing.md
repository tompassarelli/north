- A leaf is closed by its check at its landing: an issue with no checkbox and no open child is a leaf, and a lane names it with an `Item: REPO#N` trailer and no `Box:` line. `north finish` records the acceptance command that passed on the lane's tree, and safe-push puts that command on the land ticket when the tree it queues is the tree the command passed on. When the lander lands the lane it checks that the lane's commit is an ancestor of origin/main and queues a close in the issue-body outbox, which `north after-landing` drains: it reads the issue again and closes it, with a comment naming the commit and the command, only when the command on the ticket is the issue's own ``Done when: `CMD` ``. A leaf whose command has not passed on that commit (it needs the live runtime, a native client or a date, the lane was handed in with another check, or with none) stays open and is labelled `waits:runtime`. An issue with checkboxes keeps the box path of the landing tick, and an issue with an open child or one already closed is left as it is; the after-landing line names what happened to each. Why is in docs/rationale/sub-issue-cutover.md, departure 1 (north#1137).
