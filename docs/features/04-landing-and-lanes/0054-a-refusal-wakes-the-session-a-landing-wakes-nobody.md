- A refusal wakes the session, a landing wakes nobody: a ticket queued by a fleet session that does not wait for its verdict (a `finish` call or `safe-push --to main` without `--wait`) records `wakes_session` in its hand-in record. The reaper keeps that session past its closing report while the ticket is undecided. As the lander decides such a ticket, its watch on the decided areas sends a refusal to the session through the fleet, under the `[north: the lander has decided]` opener with the branch and the verdict, which starts the session's next turn within about two seconds; a landing sends nothing, the fleet tick's resume no longer wakes a session for a landed lane, and the reaper retires the reported session once its lanes are settled. The tick's resume still wakes a stopped session on a refused lane the direct send missed (north#1194).
