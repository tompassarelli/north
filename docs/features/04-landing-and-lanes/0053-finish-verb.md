- Finish: `north finish` is the call a headless fleet worker ends its lane with, offered to it as the `finish` tool beside `hand-in` (`harness/worker-tools.toml`). It runs the box's acceptance check in the lane and, only when the check passes, commits the named paths and queues the lane on the road `safe-push --to main` takes, returning at the ticket instead of waiting for the lander; a failing check commits and queues nothing and returns its output (north#1194). The call may carry the change itself, as whole files or exact replacements written into the session's own lane before the check (north#1218), and an accepted call ends the worker's turn. A launch whose brief is well specified proposes a commit message that `north finish --judge-message` scans and judges ahead, so a call that leaves its message out commits with that proposal and finds its verdict cached (north#1239).
