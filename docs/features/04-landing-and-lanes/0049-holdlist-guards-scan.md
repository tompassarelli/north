- A hold keeps its guard: `harness/holdlist.toml` lists what north must not lose, one `[[hold]]` per guarantee with its metric, budget and the test, concern, eval or land check that guards it, and `north land scan holdlist-guards-resolve BASE..HEAD`, a land check on every lane, looks each live guard up in the lane's tree (a test as `fn NAME(` in a Rust file, a concern as a `[[concern]]` in `concerns.toml`, an eval as a file under the evals directory of `harness`, a land check as a `land.check` or `land.after` line of `.safe-push`). It names each hold whose live guard the lane drops, and each hold the lane removes or turns from live to missing unless a commit says why in a `Holdlist: REASON` trailer. The scan is listed as `holdlist-guards-resolve` in `harness/gates.toml` and enforces since the 2026-11-14 review (north#1544): a guard counts as dropped only when it resolves at the merge base and is missing at the head, so a hold already stale on main refuses no lane. Why is in north#1127 and docs/rationale/holdlist.md.
