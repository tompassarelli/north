- Lander adopts finished lanes: `north land adopt` finds each lane under `~/code/<project>/worktrees/` whose tree is the one its last finish call passed on, that holds no uncommitted change, that no ticket names and whose session, and every session recorded in it, has ended by the fleet's records; it rebases the lane onto origin/main and lands it through `safe-push --to main --wait`, so a worker that retires with a finished lane needs no new session to land it. A lane that no longer rebases or that a check refuses is filed on its issue (the commit's `Item:` trailer, else the issue `north lane new` recorded) and tried no more at that head; `--dry-run` lists what it would adopt (north#1575).
