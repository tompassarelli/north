- Lane issue, archive and restore: `north lane new --issue REPO#N LANE` creates a lane under the project's `worktrees/` on a new branch from origin/main and records the issue it serves, and `north lane gc` reads that record to decide each lane's fate (north#995). A lane landed by ancestry or by content is removed. An unlanded lane or local branch that no live Fleet session, live claim or peer host holds is bundled to `~/archive/lanes/<project>/` once it has been idle 24 hours, whether or not the issue it names is still open; the bundle holds the lane's commits and, when the lane was dirty, one snapshot commit carrying its uncommitted and untracked changes, and the bundle must verify and hold that head before the worktree (build output included) and the branch go. `north lane restore BRANCH` brings the newest archived copy back to a worktree with those changes unstaged, as they were. A lane something still holds is kept and listed under its open issue, and `north lane gc --unlanded` prints that list. gc also drops the branch config of deleted branches and archives the worktree directories a failed removal leaves behind (north#1028, north#1054, north#892, north#1600).
