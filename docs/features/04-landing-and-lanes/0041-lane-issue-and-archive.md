- Lane issue and archive: `north lane new --issue REPO#N LANE` creates a lane under the project's `worktrees/` on a new branch from origin/main and records the issue it serves, and `north lane gc` reads that record to decide each lane's fate (north#995). A lane landed by ancestry or by content is removed. An unlanded lane whose issues are all closed, or that names no issue and has no owner, is bundled to `~/archive/lanes/<project>/` once it has been idle long enough, then removed; a lane on an open issue is kept and listed under that issue, and `north lane gc --unlanded` prints that list. gc also drops the branch config of deleted branches and archives the worktree directories a failed removal leaves behind (north#1028, north#1054, north#892).
