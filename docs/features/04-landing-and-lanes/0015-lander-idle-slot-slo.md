- Lander idle-slot SLO: no lane waits more than `lander_idle_slot_wait_minutes` (10) while one of the lander's `lander_check_slots` (4) check slots is idle. Each `north flow` row reads the lander queue's tickets, queued and decided under `done/`, and its `logs/`: a lane waits from the time in its ticket's name until its own precheck log, a batch candidate or speculative log, or its decision starts or ends the wait, and a check occupies a slot from the time in its log's name to the log's last write. A lane whose wait beside an idle slot passes the limit is a breach on the UTC day it passes it; `--json` lists today's under `idle_slot_wait_breaches` (lane, queue and breach times, wait minutes, idle minutes, most idle slots) with the day's count against `lander_idle_slot_breach_budget` (3) under `idle_slot_budget`, and the text row shows `idle-slot waits over 10m today N/3`. With `--stall-alarm`, the day the budget is spent sends lander-lead one `north fleet send` naming each breach, kept once per repository and day in `agents/flow/slo.json`. On 2026-11-05 lanes waited 15 to 22 minutes while check slots idled behind unleased worker builds (north#360).
