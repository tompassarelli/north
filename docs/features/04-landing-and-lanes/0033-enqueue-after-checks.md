- Checks at enqueue: a check line in `.safe-push` whose paths carry `@enqueue` also runs on the lane's own tree when the lane is queued, before its ticket is written, so a lane that fails a cheap scan is refused in the worker's turn and not after the lander's queue wait (north#697, north#842). The lines so marked are the scans that judge only the lane's diff and build nothing, such as citations, code-health layers, experiments and the `tests/it` module list. Enqueue also sorts each `sorted` block of `harness/merge-sets.toml` into the lane's last commit; the generated indexes are written by the lander alone, which refuses a lane that commits one (north#729, north#1057). Why the landing checks are split this way: docs/rationale/landing-train.md.
