- Landing flow: `north work` prints each repository's pending landings, landing rate, push-to-land latency, main idle time, oldest landing job and runner, stall verdict, project throughput, open boxes, and Actions, Wisp and Autoland capacity signals; `north accounts` prints the usage line. `north ledger flow --stall-alarm` keeps the alarm pass, and its `row` and `detect` subcommands stay machine and timer protocols. The open count the goal needs closed in project throughput weights each open `release` or `bet` issue by its `size:S`, `size:M` or `size:L` label as 1, 2 or 4, and leaves out an issue with no workable box. Open boxes print as `boxes workable W waiting V decision D` (each unticked `- [ ]` line of an open issue, nested ones included; waiting while an `after DATE`, `(not before DATE)` or `(waits: DATE, ...)` window has not opened, decision when it carries `(decision: Tom: ...)`). `--stall-alarm` sends notices to the active Fleet parent and the proxy, and mentions Tom after 60 minutes until main moves and jobs recover. Fleet sessions use the same one-hour landing WIP limit (floor 2), with landing-path and `CASE=URGENT` exceptions.
