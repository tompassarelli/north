- Landing refusals in shadow: only a broken build, clippy, failing tests, the pick itself (a conflict or dropped commits), a secret or a privacy leak (deny-list, commit email, native item references) refuses a lane; every other landing gate (test admission, test integrity, the incident test, tool removal, the policy sweep, the language, build-cache, dependency and stub-exec gates, bin references, strategy replay, commit trailers and the revert guard) follows `landing_refusals` in `harness/defaults.toml`, and under `shadow` it appends its would-be refusal to `~/.local/state/north/gates/landing-shadow.jsonl` on the host that judged it (`at` is `enqueue`, `pick` or `candidate`), warns on stderr and lets the lane land; `safe-push --dry-run` prints the same would-be refusals and passes. `north land gate-stats` prints those rows in its shadow section, so each gate earns refusal back with a would-be refusal that names a real fault.
