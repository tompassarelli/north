- `safe-push --to main` lands the lane as one commit: before its land checks and the Autoland queue push, `north lane squash` folds the lane's unpublished commits (none an origin ref reaches) into one commit on the newest published commit, with the first commit's author, every trailer kept once and the message from `--message FILE` or the lane's last commit; `--several-commits REASON` keeps them apart and logs to `several-commit-landings.tsv` (docs/rationale/one-landing-per-change.md). A lane whose landing subject starts with WIP or says the work is not written is refused with a request for `--message FILE`.
