- Landing-path lanes replayed under their own machinery: `north land machinery-replay BASE..HEAD [--repo DIR]...`, run from the lane's own build of a lane that changes a file of `landing_path_paths` in harness/defaults.toml (safe-push, the lander, land checks, scans and gates, the land offload and its jail, the worker and land sandboxes, depbox, the head-north runtime), replays the last 3 recorded rounds of each named queue through `north land replay`, the replay `north land promote` runs, with the lane's build as the checker. A round the lane's build replays unlike its record is replayed again under the lander's runtime. The lane is refused when it turns a verdict into no verdict, when a case that passes under main's machinery fails under its own, or when it moves a round between landed and refused and its diff adds no `Replay-flip: ROUND :: WHY` line for that round to docs/replay-flips.md, which its reviewers accept with the patch; a moved round that main's machinery reaches no verdict on gives the check no verdict. A lane outside the list, or whose list files compile into no build of its own, is not replayed. It reads the lander link, the recorded tickets and the replay worktrees, so it runs outside the candidate confinement (north#1537).
