- Loop registry (north#112): `harness/loops.toml` names every automatic decision north makes (tier, effort, ETA, budget, capacity estimate, pressure band, check deadline, placement, worker concurrency, landing batch, skill routing) with the files that make it, its outcome signal, its updater, its cadence and the record under the state root whose newest write is its last update. `north loops check` prints each loop's last update and fails one with no outcome signal, naming the box that owns it, or one whose record is older than twice its cadence; each host checks only the loops whose records it holds and lists the rest as `checked on ROLE`, `north loops check --role ROLE` checks one role, `north tune` prints the check after its daily tick on the ledger host, and the laptop's hourly north-tempo timer runs `north loops check --role lander`. A bin test fails when a decision site behind dispatch, fleet launch, capacity admission or the lander is missing from the registry.
