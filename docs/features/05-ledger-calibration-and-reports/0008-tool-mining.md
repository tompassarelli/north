- Tool mining: `north tool-mine` reads the last 7 days of Bash tool calls through `north convo`, turns each Bash call into one step named by its first working program and subcommand (calls made only of read-only filters such as `head`, `rg` or `jq`, and shell loops, are not steps), and files on north#112 the top 5 (`--max N`) maximal sequences of 3 to 8 steps that 5 or more sessions ran and that include a subcommanded program or `ssh`, ranked by sessions times steps, naming the sequence, its session count and the `north` subcommand it should become; `--dry-run` prints those patterns and the count it dropped without filing, each box is filed once per sequence through the detectors' filer, and its spend lands in the ledger as the `recurring-sequence` detector.
