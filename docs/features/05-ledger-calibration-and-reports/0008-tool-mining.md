- Tool mining: `north tool-mine` reads the last 7 days of Bash tool calls through `north convo` and files on north#112 the top 5 (`--max N`) sequences of 3 to 8 steps that 5 or more sessions ran, naming each sequence, its session count and the `north` subcommand it should become. Each Bash call becomes one step named by its first working program and subcommand (calls made only of read-only filters such as `head`, `rg` or `jq`, and shell loops, are not steps). A sequence is maximal, includes a subcommanded program or `ssh`, and ranks by sessions times steps. `--dry-run` prints those patterns and the count it dropped without filing; each box is filed once per sequence through the detectors' filer, and its spend lands in the ledger as the `recurring-sequence` detector.
