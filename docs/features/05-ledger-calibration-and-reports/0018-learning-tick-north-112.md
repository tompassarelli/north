- Learning tick (north#112): `north tune` decides, from recently closed boxes, the cells briefs and routing read: `route CATEGORY` (the arm dispatch takes once both arms hold enough runs) and `eta_minutes` and `budget_usd` per family and category. It writes them to `~/.local/state/north/tune/cells.json`, which dispatch and `north fleet launch` follow while fresh, and logs each change with its evidence to `provenance.jsonl`. It prints a `rose` line for each cell whose cost per closed box rose sharply week on week (north#579). It prints, per tier and category, the median and p90 ratio of actual to estimated minutes and cost of the week's runs, the share inside their estimate (`n/a` for cost without an expected spend); a cell counts as drifted when its ratio at the quantile its estimator covers (the ETA's `eta_coverage`, the Budget's p90) left 0.67-1.5x over three or more runs while an estimate replayed for each run from the same quantile of the minutes or cost of its cell's runs that ended in the 7 days before it started would have sat inside it: a stale estimate, not a noisy cell (north#35). It prints a `drifted` line for each drifted cell (north#522). Neither alarm is kept as an issue, since a week-window reading cannot clear inside a day: the tick tells the proxy once whenever the set of risen or drifted cells changes, clearing included, and keeps the set it told in `alarms.json` beside the cells (north#1488). Beside it the tick prints `outside the band under every estimator: N cells` and lists them, a count rather than an alarm, so a reader knows where an estimate is worth little, and counts the cells outside the band whose runs held too few earlier runs to replay; those are neither drifted nor counted. The band is not widened for noise and no cell is dropped. It prints the share of the last day's runs that recorded each essential field and fails when one is under 98% (north#255). An `eta_minutes` or `budget_usd` cell moves at most 2x from the value the cells file already holds in one tick, on top of reading the week's median, so one bad day of harness failures cannot swing it (on 2026-11-07 a single tick raised the sonnet tooling budget from 3.57 to 19.0); the change's provenance records the figure's limit. `--dry-run` writes and tells nothing. Budget scoring: each worker run stores the Budget its brief set at launch (the `at $X API-equivalent spend` line drawn at E's p90) in `runs.budget_usd`, kept through completion. `north tune` prints, over 7 d, the runs carrying a stored Budget and per category n, the share of runs over their Budget and the actual/E p50 and p90; once a category holds 30 such runs its `budget_usd` cells take their multiplier from that p90 (times the multiplier the Budgets were set with, read back as the median Budget over the median spend, held between 1x and 4x because most runs spend pennies and their p90 is a few runs' tail) instead of the constant 2x.
