A worker launch registers its name on the ledger host in the transaction that claims it. `north fleet tick` writes the current trips to `~/.local/state/agents/spend/trips.json` from the five-hour readings and each running session's spend, appends new ones to `trips.jsonl` and tells the proxy once per trip. `north spend [--json]` prints the reserve and any trip, then the waste of the last 24 h: needs workers recorded with `north fleet need`, launches placed beside a holder (the `dup:` note earlier sessions recorded), and revert tickets withdrawn as false, each unmeasured with its reason when its record is unread. `agents plan` orders candidates by weekly room per hour to the reset and skips any at a provider limit.
