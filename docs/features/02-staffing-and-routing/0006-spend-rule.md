- Spend rule (north#702, north#1076, docs/rationale/spend-rule.md): north holds back no launch on usage grounds; only the provider's own limit and the declared operational reserve stop a new worker, and each refusal names which. A worker launch registers its name on the ledger host in the transaction that claims it. `north spend [--json]` prints the week points left across accounts and the worker sessions working, then the waste of the last 24 h: needs workers recorded with `north fleet need`, launches placed beside a holder (the `dup:` note earlier sessions recorded), and revert tickets withdrawn as false, each unmeasured with its reason when its record is unread. `agents plan` orders candidates by weekly room per hour to the reset and skips any at a provider limit.
