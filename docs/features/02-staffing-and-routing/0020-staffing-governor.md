- Staffing governor (north#112): after placement, `north fleet launch` refuses a lane its repository's lander cannot absorb with `refuse: staffing: ...` and tells the parent `Stop: landing`. It refuses when queued lanes reach what the busy-hour landing rate drains in `staffing_drain_minutes` (30, at least 2), or when the oldest ticket, aged from its `queued_at` or mtime, waited over twice that plus 7 minutes (`lander stalled`). A queue no lander has recorded a round or decision for is not governed. In-flight load and host CPU pressure run in shadow as `would_refuse`. Each decision is one line in `fleet-governor/decisions.jsonl`. Leads, research, `lander-*`, `main is red` fixes, `Main-red:` briefs, `--test` and `--take-over` are exempt; a host that is not `landing.lander-host` allows and logs it; `--staffing-ok REASON` overrides and is recorded, except from a proxy-role session, whose override is refused; under a hold, a launch whose brief names the ticket at the front of the land queue (its file stem, such as `1762400000000000-fix-lander`) starts as `front-of-queue` (north#636). `north dispatch` records a staffing refusal as a 5-minute hold, not a failed launch.
