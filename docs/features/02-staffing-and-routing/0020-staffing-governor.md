- Staffing governor (north#112): after placement picks the machine, `north fleet launch` refuses a lane its repository's lander cannot absorb, with `refuse: staffing: ...`, and tells the parent `Stop: landing` so it waits for the queue to drain. It reads the land queue's files directly (`land-queue/<key>/*.json` and `done/`, not the hour-stale `land_tempo` rows), takes the rate as the larger of the last day's landings per busy hour (hours with one or more landings) and the last hour's, and falls back to the all-repos rate for a repository with fewer than 10 landings in 24 h. It enforces two checks: the queue (refuse when queued lanes reach the larger of the lanes the rate drains in `staffing_drain_minutes`, 30, and 2) and a stall (refuse when the oldest pending ticket waited over twice the drain bound plus 7 minutes, printed as `lander stalled, oldest N min`). It computes in shadow, refusing nothing on them, the in-flight load by Little's law (queued plus `staffing_lanes_per_session`, 1.18, times working sessions, admits from the last 3 minutes that are not running yet, and the launch, against the lanes the rate absorbs over the drain bound plus `staffing_launch_to_queued_minutes`, 66; 4 lanes when nothing landed in a day) and the chosen host's protected CPU pressure against `admit_psi_attended_high`. Each decision, with its numbers and `would_refuse`, is one JSON line in `~/.local/state/north/fleet-governor/decisions.jsonl` under a lock, which is the loop's outcome signal. Leads, research boxes, `lander-*` launches, a task that is the open `main is red` issue, `--test` and `--take-over` are not governed; a host that is not the committed `landing.lander-host` allows and logs `governor: no lander state on this host`; `--staffing-ok REASON` starts anyway and is recorded on the session. `north dispatch` records a staffing refusal as a 5-minute hold, not a failed launch, so it counts toward no failure hold, retry, proxy hand-off or `launch_failures` breach.
