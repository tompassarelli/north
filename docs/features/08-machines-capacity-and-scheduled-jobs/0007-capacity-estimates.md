- Capacity estimates: `machine-capacity estimate -- CMD ARG...` predicts a command's cores, peak memory, I/O and disk busy share from its newest matching measured runs (`capacity_sizing_runs`), falling back from the exact signature through owner, family and repository to the declared class, and prints the match level and confidence (`capacity_high_confidence_runs`). An expensive target with no history of its own stays heavy at low confidence. Admission reserves from these estimates with `capacity_memory_margin`, and status and probe publish the committed totals the spawn gate reads.
