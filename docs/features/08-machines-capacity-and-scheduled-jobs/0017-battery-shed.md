- Battery shed: while the laptop runs on battery, each `north capacity laptop-auto` tick holds its placement as draining, freezes heavy batch leases and lowers the CPU quota of `agent.slice`, and the first tick on mains power undoes all three; a charge controller's brief discharging flicker counts as mains (north#834). The same tick caps `agent.slice` at the lowest of the mode's quota, the battery quota and the hot-CPU quota, whose values are in `harness/defaults.toml`. `north fleet launch --local` is refused on battery and names the machines that take the box instead, and the last battery alert warns at the critical level that the machine is about to power off (north#1140).
