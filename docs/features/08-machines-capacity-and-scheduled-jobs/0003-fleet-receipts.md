- Fleet receipts: `north fleet launch` makes the calling session the new session's parent, or the `proxy` session for a launch from outside the fleet, unless `--parent` says otherwise. Upward messages are by exception (north#909): when a child ends a turn that landed a commit, opens Done, Split or Blocked, holds an open gate, or ends while it has no unlanded ticket in the land queue, no registered wait and no `Stop: landing` or `Stop: wait until` line of its own, its host queues one `[from NAME · turn N · exit E · landed SHA]` message with the reply to the parent — to the proxy only the reply's first three lines, a later `Stop:` line in place of the third, with `north fleet show NAME` for the evidence — unless those facts (opening word, landed sha from the push log, open gates from the ledger, waiting) repeat the last report that went up; other turns stay in `north fleet show NAME` without waking it, a failed turn arrives with its transcript tail and a host death arrives urgently. A gate the child opens reaches the parent as one line; one opened with `--issue` holds that issue from dispatch and launches until `north gate answer` closes it and the child receives a `[gate ID]` message. The ledger claims each notice once, so a restart never resends one, and `--test` sessions keep their gates out of Tom's digest.
