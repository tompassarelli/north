- Fleet receipts: `north fleet launch` makes the calling session the new session's parent, or the `proxy` session for a launch from outside the fleet, unless `--parent` says otherwise. When a child ends a turn that landed a commit or whose reply opens Done, Not done, Split, Stop or Blocked, its host queues one `[from NAME · turn N · exit E · landed SHA]` message with the reply to the parent; other turns stay in `north fleet show NAME` without waking it, a failed turn arrives with its transcript tail and a host death arrives urgently. A gate the child opens reaches the parent as one line; one opened with `--issue` holds that issue from dispatch and launches until `north gate answer` closes it and the child receives a `[gate ID]` message. The ledger claims each notice once, so a restart never resends one, and `--test` sessions keep their gates out of Tom's digest.
