- Queued laptop jobs: `threads queue add PLACEMENT REF|- -- CMD...` queues work for a machine that may be asleep; `threads queue [--by POLLER] take PLACEMENT[,PLACEMENT...]` claims the oldest job of those placements atomically and prints it as JSON, recording POLLER (the runner passes its hostname) as the latest poller of that placement list as a `queue-poll:PLACEMENTS` job run; `threads queue msg ID done|failed TEXT` records the result and comments it on the job's issue (a note on a native item); `threads queue msg ID requeued TEXT` puts a taken job back at its original position as `requeued`, which `take` claims like `queued`; `threads queue drop ID REASON` drops a queued or requeued job (taken, done and failed jobs are refused), recording REASON as its result and a ledger note; `threads queue` lists jobs with their wait from queued to taken (to now while queued) and their `queue-ID` job run's host, start and rc, and after the table names for each job queued 5 minutes or more which host last polled this ledger for its placement and when, or that none has (the `waiting` field of `--json`, which `north laptop status` appends for the laptop). `north laptop-runner` (laptop timer each minute and on wake) takes jobs for `$LAPTOP_RUNNER_PLACEMENT` (default `laptop`; nexus runs it as `nexus`), plus the shared `pool` placement while on AC power (or with no battery) and `machine-capacity probe --class heavy` would grant a heavy lease, from its own ledger and then from every other fleet machine with an `ssh` destination in orchestration.toml (`ssh HOST north threads`, so a ledger host that cannot reach the laptop or Nexus still has its jobs taken; north#330), one at a time under flock and runs each through `job-run queue-ID` (`queue-HOST-ID` for another machine's ledger) in its own process group, keeping its full output at `~/.local/state/laptop-runner/queue-ID.log` on the host that ran it (the ledger result names host and path, plus the last 3000 characters). Laptop availability: `north laptop open|close|drain|toggle [until HH:MM] [REASON...]`, `north laptop status [--json]` and `north laptop tick` are the single writer of `~/.local/state/placement/laptop.json` (`{state, since, reason, by, until?, grace?}`, written atomically with a ledger note on an `ops` item) on the ledger host, forwarding over ssh where `~/.config/threads/remote` exists; `open` is available, `close` drains with a 2-minute grace and `drain` with 15 minutes (`LAPTOP_GRACE` seconds overrides), and a word holds until its `until`, else until the next window edge. The `laptop-window` timer runs `north laptop tick` each minute on Nexus: it opens at the window's open time and drains at its close (`/etc/placement/laptop-window.json`: tz, open, close), returns to the window state when a word's `until` passes, and turns draining into unavailable once no laptop job is taken. With placement `laptop`, `north laptop-runner` takes nothing unless the state is available (read through `north laptop status --json`, keeping the last cached state while the ledger host is unreachable), and past a drain's grace it SIGTERMs the running job's process group and records it `requeued`. `north fleet tree` (second line) and `agents flow` (first line) print `north laptop status`.
