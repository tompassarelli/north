- Seat stall breaker: `north capacity seat-probe`, a user unit in the owner's own slice on the laptop, writes and syncs a small file under `~/.local/state/north/seat-probe` every 2 s and logs how long each sync took, one JSON line a sample in two files of at most 4 MiB. Once a sync has been out for `seat_stall_trip_seconds` (harness/defaults.toml), still blocked, the probe stops the fleet's units on the laptop listed in `seat_stall_units`, records the stop with the units that were running and sends the coordinator a page; the units stay stopped until `north capacity seat-stall resume` starts them, which refuses while a sync is out past the trip time. `north capacity seat-stall --since DURATION` prints each stall over 1 s with its length, the fleet units running then and whether and when the breaker stopped them, and exits 0 when it could read the log. Placement keeps a new session off the laptop while its longest synced write of the last minute is past the trip time. It replaced a 10 s disk pressure average and the 50 MiB/s relief cap on agent.slice, which let 83 stalls of 6 s or more through in one hour (north#1553).
