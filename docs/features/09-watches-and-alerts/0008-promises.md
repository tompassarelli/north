- Promises: a line of a session's report that names a time ahead of the report (`2:16 AM` in the laptop window's zone, or `2026-11-08T02:16Z`) and a command of two or more words in backticks is kept in the fleet root's `promises.jsonl`, with the parent the report went to, when the turn ends. When that time comes, a host's fleet tick records the promise in `promises-checked.jsonl` and sends the parent one urgent message naming the session, the line, the due time and the quoted command, for the parent to check; the fleet runs nothing from report text (south#116). A time before the report reads as a statement of the past and becomes no message.
