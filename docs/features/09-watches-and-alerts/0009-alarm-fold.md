- Alarm fold: `north fleet send` from an alarm service (north-health, north-land-watch, north-ops-flow, north-capacity-watchdog, north-lease-reaper, north-runtime, and the lander watchdog's `north land watchdog:` pages) delivers one message per cause and recipient within 30 minutes and holds the repeats for `north fleet digest`, printing that it held them (north#909, north#1019, north#1031). Only the page-now classes reach the proxy: a red main, a runtime rollback, a disk past its watermark, a security finding, a health unit alarm and a land-watch "not live" page its follower check let through; every other alarm waits in the digest. A cause is the alarm's first sentence with its numbers blanked, after the ops watch's `still, N min:` prefix, so a re-sent overload whose load moved is the same cause; a notice that the alarm cleared (`: recovered`, `north ops watch: cleared after`, `harness unit X recovered:`) opens the cause again and is held for the digest too, since it asks nothing of its recipient, except a health unit's one cleared line, which goes out when the unit recovers. The sending host keeps the window in `alarm-folds.json` beside the fleet's state, a held message goes to `held.jsonl` there, and a folded alarm is left out of `alarms.jsonl`. north-health's own unit-failure notice pages once per unit and its first error line and holds the same key for six hours, so a unit that keeps failing reaches the fold once per six hours. Every other sender, landing notices included, passes unchanged.
