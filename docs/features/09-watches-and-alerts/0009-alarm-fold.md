- Alarm fold: `north fleet send` from an alarm service (north-health, north-land-watch, north-ops-flow, north-capacity-watchdog, north-lease-reaper, and the lander watchdog's `north land watchdog:` pages) delivers one message per cause and recipient within 30 minutes and drops the repeats, printing that it folded them (north#909). A cause is the alarm's first sentence with its numbers blanked, after the ops watch's `still, N min:` prefix, so a re-sent overload whose load moved is the same cause; a notice that the alarm cleared (`: recovered`, `north ops watch: cleared after`) always passes and opens the cause again. The sending host keeps the window in `alarm-folds.json` beside the fleet's state, and a folded alarm is left out of `alarms.jsonl`. Every other sender, landing notices included, passes unchanged.
