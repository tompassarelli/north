## Source hierarchy

Prefer specifications, maintained project documentation, source at a named
revision, first-party engineering accounts, and reproducible measurements.
Recency matters for active ecosystems; operational history matters for mature
infrastructure. Local conventions are candidates, not proof that they remain
the right choice.
