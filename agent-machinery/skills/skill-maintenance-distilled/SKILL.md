# Skill maintenance

Admit explicit lasting-rule requests, recurring stable lessons, or emphasized
future constraints only. Treat phrases such as `durable lesson`, `durable
policy`, `learning lesson`, `remember this`, `never again`, `fleet-wide`, `by
construction`, and clear equivalents as explicit maintenance requests; never
require the operator to separately say “edit a skill.” Intensity raises
priority, not scope. Reject one-off direction, personal facts, incident
preference, or non-reusable frustration.

During a live workflow, harvest a successful move when a bounded wave proves it
reusable and it would change a future decision. Under an explicit maintenance
request, update the owning policy before settlement without turning maintenance
into a release prerequisite or preserving an incident-specific patch as a
universal rule. Repeated wins may establish the same lesson without special
keywords; one merely plausible tactic does not.

Extract trigger, rule, scope, compliant move, and escalation; preserve force,
not abuse, chronology, dates, or justification. Amend authority first and remove
duplicates atomically. Keep universal boundaries always loaded and workflows
triggerable. Create a skill only without an owner; enforce only mechanically
decidable rules. Never edit projections.

Validate a durable positive, episodic negative, standard validator, and nearest
fixture. Publish normally; prove consumer→published authority before claiming
retention.

`agents path skill-maintenance-reference` and read its `SKILL.md` completely.
