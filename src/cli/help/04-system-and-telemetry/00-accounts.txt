usage: north accounts [--json] | north accounts reset [--dry-run]
reset reads the coordinator's account and Fable meter, checks the cached usage reset grants, and appends one line to ~/.local/state/north/usage/resets.jsonl with the account, time, meter before and after, the grants and the outcome. It never clicks a reset: Anthropic's Consumer Terms forbid automating claude.ai and no provider reset command or API exists, so the grant stays for Tom (docs/rationale/usage-resets.md).
