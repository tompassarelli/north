# Nintendo: Melee

Read before padbridge work or any Melee work in Mak'gora that takes a number, a frame count or an animation from the game. It adds Nintendo's terms to the rules in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). No exception to SKILL.md's rules exists for Melee.

## Never

- Read values from Melee's disc files, or from game binaries, to decide behaviour, because behaviour must come from observation and public facts, not extracted data.
- Use Nintendo's code, art, models, textures, sounds, music, data tables, script bodies or text, because they belong to Nintendo; animation and art come from our own work.

## Where facts come from

- Black-box observation of the game as a player sees it, and map-side instrumentation, as in SKILL.md.
- Public documentation and public community data: SmashWiki, Smashboards and public frame-data sites. Cite them and record facts in our own words as our design targets, so our sources are traceable and our expression is our own.
