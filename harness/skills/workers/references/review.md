# Review

Read this before you land policy prose or hook changes, staff a planner or adversarial reviewers, or land a `Spec: judged` cross-module or cross-repo lane.

- Policy prose and hook changes (the paths `safe-push --durable-harness-paths <repository>` prints; in north skills, hooks, agent definitions, agent-machinery and AGENTS.md, not `bin/` or `harness/lib/` code) land only with a `Reviewed: <verdict>; reviewer: <reviewer session>; patch-id <id>` trailer from a fresh reviewer who did not author the change (review-gate refuses a reviewer named as the session that landed it, and the landing records the reviewer in tasks.review) (a Claude reviewer of a different tier from the author), because authors miss their own drift. The reviewer reads the final diff and prints the id from `north review-gate id origin/main HEAD $(safe-push --durable-harness-paths <repository>)` run in the lane; the author copies it into the trailer. safe-push refuses a landing whose covered diff has another id, so an edit after the review needs a new review; a rebase keeps the id unless main changed a line next to the change. Policy text Tom dictated lands with `Reviewed: Tom (dictated YYYY-MM-DD); patch-id <id>` and no agent reviewer, because no reviewer overrules his own words.
- Before staffing a planner's architectural design, have one fresh Claude reviewer of a different tier (Opus for a Sonnet author, Sonnet for an Opus author) try to break it, and revise once, because an author shares its own blind spots.
- Before landing, an independent review checks the work against Tom's intent and cuts formal machinery the spec does not ask for. Before landing a `Spec: judged` cross-module or cross-repo lane, a fresh Claude reviewer of a different tier checks the diff for wrong behavior and weakened tests; fix what it finds before `safe-push`.
- Read [planner effort](notes.md#planner-effort) before staffing `planner`, and [review lenses](notes.md#review-lenses-and-findings) before staffing adversarial reviewers or weighing their findings.
